# 非阻塞的前存资格与事实粒度

以下问题不影响本轮 4 项 JSR 的工作判断：相关旧句在 after 原样保留，或分开和合并两种计数下均保留。它们仍可能影响以后 KPR 的唯一事实分母；不要求为得到 JSR 结果重复确认。

## 目标 7：求职目标中的关联

- 用户原话 7/5：`It's crucial for me to find a new job that offers a better work-life balance and aligns with my humanitarian goals.`
- 用户原话 7/7：`I'm really looking forward to finding a position that supports both my career goals and my personal health.`
- before 与 after 均有：`Looking for a new job with better work-life balance`；`Career goals are aligned with humanitarian values`；`Envisions ideal role as one contributing to healthcare initiatives while sustaining a balanced and fulfilling life`。after 另有 `Has transitioned to a new role with healthier work-life balance`。
- **以后可判断的问题：**这些旧句及其上下文是否足以在更新前完整表达“同一求职目标兼顾人道目标、职业目标与个人健康”，还是只能分别认定已存的较窄诉求？不必把医疗倡议直接等同人道目标，也不必将平衡生活直接等同个人健康。旧句前后未变；历史上的求职与此后已转岗可结合上下文，不推断此后一定不再求职。[目标 7 完整证据](../williams_native_prefix5_20261028_v3/development_diagnosis_v1/target_07_evidence.json)

## 目标 12/13：自我照顾与平衡

- 用户原话 12/5、13/3：`Prioritizing self-care and seeking support from friends and colleagues helps me maintain balance.`
- before 与 after 同为：`Prioritizes self-care and seeks support from friends and colleagues to manage stress`。
- **以后可判断的问题：**“管理压力”是否足以等价表达用户说的“维持平衡”，还是后者的关系在 before 未完整保存？不能仅凭主题相近就要求按完整关系算作前存。无论按哪一种判断，已存原句均未发生变化。[目标 12 完整证据](../williams_native_prefix5_20261028_v3/development_diagnosis_v1/target_12_evidence.json)

## 目标 10：宽泛职业和具体岗位

- 用户原话 10/1–2：`I am currently Employed, working in the film industry. I work at Historical Productions Ltd. as a Director.`
- before 与 after 均有：`Is a filmmaker`；`Works at Historical Productions Ltd. as a Director`。after 另有 `Recently promoted to lead director at Historical Productions Ltd`。
- **以后可判断的问题：**`filmmaker` 是独立职业事实，还是与本会话的 Director 岗位按主 KPR 粒度归并？两条记忆都保留，不能因为分母粒度未定就判联合失败。[目标 10 完整证据](../williams_native_prefix5_20261028_v3/development_diagnosis_v1/target_10_evidence.json)
